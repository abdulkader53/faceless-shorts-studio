### A. The YouTube Shorts Revenue Share Model
Since February 2023, Shorts monetization operates on a **Creator Pool System**:
1. All ad revenue from ads served between Shorts is collected in a regional pool.
2. Music licensing fees are deducted.
3. Creators receive **45%** of their allocated pool based on their proportion of total regional views.

## 4. Algorithmic Retention Architecture

To achieve millions of views, a Short must satisfy two strict algorithmic metrics:

```
[Impression in Feed]
         │
         ▼
[Metric 1: Viewed vs. Swiped Away (VVSA)]  ── Must exceed 75% - 85%
         │
         ▼
[Metric 2: Average Percentage Viewed (APV)] ── Must exceed 110% - 140%
         │
         ▼
[Algorithmic Viral Expansion: 500k -> 2M -> 10M+]
```

### The 3 Core Retention Laws:
1. **The 2-Second Non-Fluff Rule**: Never say *"Today we are looking at..."*. Start with the paradox: *"In 1859, two world superpowers mobilized battleships... over a single pig."*
2. **The 2.0-Second Visual Cut Pace**: The brain disengages if a static graphic remains on screen for longer than 2.5 seconds. Every line in our scripts triggers a new camera angle, zoom, or visual reveal.
3. **The Seamless Infinite Loop**: The final sentence must syntactically lead back into the opening hook sentence. Viewers watch the first 3 seconds a second time before realizing the video restarted, driving APV to **130%+**!

### Why 90% of "AI Faceless" Channels Get Banned / Demonetized:
YouTube's updated **"Inauthentic Content"** and **"Reused Content"** policies target:
1. Stolen gameplay footage (e.g., Minecraft parkour, GTA cars) with AI voiceover.
